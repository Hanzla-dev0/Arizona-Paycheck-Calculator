A pay stub looks confusing at first, but it follows one simple idea: **gross pay minus taxes minus deductions equals net pay.**

## 1. Gross pay
This is what you earned before anything is taken out. Salary workers see a fixed amount. Hourly workers see hours times rate, plus overtime if any.

## 2. Taxes
- **Federal income tax** is based on your Form W-4.
- **State income tax (AZ)** is based on your Arizona Form A-4 percentage. If you never filed one, 2.0% is the default.
- **Social Security (OASDI)** is 6.2% of wages up to the yearly limit.
- **Medicare** is 1.45% of wages.

## 3. Deductions
Pre-tax items such as health insurance or a traditional 401(k) lower your taxable wages. After-tax items such as a Roth 401(k) or garnishments do not.

## 4. Year-to-date (YTD) column
YTD totals add up everything since January 1. They help you check that Social Security stops at the yearly limit and that your total tax matches your expectations.

## Check your own numbers
Enter your gross pay and deductions in the [Arizona paycheck calculator](/) and compare the result with your stub. Small differences are normal, but a big gap is worth asking your payroll department about. To change state withholding, read the [Form A-4 guide](/guides/arizona-form-a-4-withholding/).
