Payroll deductions can be taken before or after certain taxes. Traditional workplace retirement contributions generally reduce federal income-tax wages but are usually still subject to Social Security and Medicare. Qualifying cafeteria-plan health premiums may reduce additional wage bases.

## Practical steps

Check the benefit plan documents and pay stub labels rather than assuming every deduction is pre-tax. The treatment of Roth contributions, insurance and other benefits can differ.
