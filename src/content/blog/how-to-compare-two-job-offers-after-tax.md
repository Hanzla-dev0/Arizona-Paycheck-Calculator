A higher salary does not always produce a proportionally higher paycheck. Benefits, retirement contributions, work location, pay frequency and withholding elections all affect the amount deposited into your account.

## Practical steps

Compare both offers using the same filing status and pay schedule. Include health premiums, retirement contributions, bonuses and commuting-related costs separately. Treat payroll estimates as planning figures and confirm benefit costs with each employer.
