Paycheck withholding is an estimate of tax paid during the year, not the final tax bill. Federal income tax withholding follows Form W-4 information, while Arizona employees generally make a state withholding election using Form A-4. Social Security and Medicare are separate payroll taxes.

## Practical steps

Start with your gross pay and pay frequency. Review your W-4 filing status and any additional withholding. For Arizona, compare the A-4 percentage on file with the amount shown on your pay statement. A higher or lower withholding election changes take-home pay but does not by itself change your final tax liability.
