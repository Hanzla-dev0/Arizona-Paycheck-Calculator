Hourly pay is commonly calculated from an hourly rate and hours worked. Salary pay is usually expressed as an annual amount and divided across the employer’s pay periods. Overtime eligibility depends on job duties and applicable wage laws, not simply whether a worker is paid a salary.

## Practical steps

When estimating hourly income, use a realistic average of hours and identify overtime separately. A paycheck can vary from period to period when hours, tips, commissions or unpaid time off change.
