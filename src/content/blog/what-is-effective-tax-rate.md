title: "What Is an Effective Tax Rate?"
description: "Understand the difference between marginal tax brackets, average tax rates and paycheck withholding."
pubDate: 2026-11-10
---

A marginal tax rate applies to the next dollar of taxable income within a bracket. An effective tax rate compares tax paid with a broader income amount, so it is usually lower than the top marginal rate. Withholding percentages on a paycheck are not necessarily the same as either measure.

## Practical steps

For a useful comparison, define the numerator and denominator clearly: federal income tax only, total payroll taxes, or combined federal, state and local taxes. Then compare like-for-like income periods.
